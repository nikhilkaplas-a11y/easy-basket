About Us

Welcome to Sandhal Clinic & Panchkarma Centre

Established in 1991, Sandhal Clinic & Panchkarma Centre has been a trusted name in holistic healthcare, blending ancient Ayurveda with modern expertise. For over three decades we have delivered personalized, natural healing for a wide range of conditions.

We specialize in Ayurvedic Internal Medicine (Kayachikitsa), Panchkarma, and advanced para-surgical procedures including Kshaar Sutra, Kshaar Karma, and Agni Karma—especially for anorectal disorders.

Led by Dr. Vivek Sandhal (BAMS, M.D. Kayachikitsa) and supported by Dr. Pooja Verma (MBBS), our team focuses on root-cause treatment and compassionate, patient-centred care.
