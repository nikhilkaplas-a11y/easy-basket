Easy Basket – Account and data deletion

You can request permanent deletion of your Easy Basket account and associated data. This page explains how.

Steps to request account deletion

  1. Open the Easy Basket app on your device.
  2. Go to Profile (or Account / Settings).
  3. Find Delete account or Request account deletion.
  4. Follow the in-app steps and confirm.
  5. Alternatively, email us at support@easybasket.in from the email or phone linked to your account and state: “I request deletion of my Easy Basket account and associated data.” Include the phone number registered with your account.

What we delete

After we process your request, we will delete or anonymise:

  • Your account (profile, name, email, phone)
  • Saved delivery addresses
  • Order history linked to your account
  • Any preferences and saved data in our systems

What we may keep (and for how long)

We may retain the following only as required by law or for legitimate business purposes:

  • Transaction and payment records: For tax, accounting, or dispute resolution, as required by law (typically up to 7 years or as per local law).
  • Anonymised or aggregated data: Data that no longer identifies you may be kept for analytics and improvement.

We do not retain your personal data longer than necessary for these purposes.

How long deletion takes

We aim to process account deletion requests within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation once your account and associated data have been deleted (subject to the exceptions above).
