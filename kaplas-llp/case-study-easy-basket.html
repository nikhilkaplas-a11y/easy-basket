The brief

A delivery platform built for tier-2 India.

Build a hyperlocal grocery delivery platform from scratch — designed for tier-2 Indian cities, with strong COD support, multilingual interfaces, and reliable delivery in patchy-network areas.

The catch: ship three coordinated apps (customer, rider, merchant) and a backend that holds them all together — without burning months of runway.

Four hard problems we solved.

Three apps, one source of truth

Customer, rider, merchant — each with different needs but all working off the same order state. Required a careful state machine and event-driven sync across clients.

Real-time rider tracking

Rider location updates every 5 seconds, broadcast to customers watching their order. Solved with WebSocket-backed pub/sub on Redis and exponential back-off on weak networks.

COD reconciliation without errors

Cash-on-delivery means physical cash flows we have to track. Dedicated reconciliation service with rider wallet ledgers, so no rupee goes unaccounted for.

OTP-verified delivery

Originally FCM-push, swapped to Twilio SMS at launch for higher reliability. 99.4% first-time OTP delivery now — even in low-network zones.

Live, profitable, and growing.

Easy Basket runs in production today, processing hundreds of orders per day with 99.4% OTP delivery and a 22-minute average fulfillment time. The COD reconciliation system has yet to lose a single rupee.

More importantly, the architecture handles 3× growth without changes — proving that getting the foundation right pays off when scale arrives.
